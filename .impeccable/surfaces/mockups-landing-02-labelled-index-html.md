# Surface: zapzapsites.com landing (mockup 02, Labelled)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: owner-operators of small local businesses, non-technical. Action: two equal actions, join the waitlist or use an invite code. English first with EN/ES switch. Sample businesses only, labelled as samples. Own JS allowed with no-JS fallback. Anti-goals: generic SaaS, corporate, techy, playful.

Open: AI detection is not live in production; the "we work out what should be editable" claim is kept from the current apex copy pending confirmation.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The customer's existing site gets labelled like a shop shelf: every strip of embossed tape marks a part that becomes editable. Refuses the category default of headline left, editor screenshot right, three feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cool light workbench grey ground. Embossed label tape as the only colour: red for text, black for contact facts, blue for lists, green for photos, yellow (black letters) for hours; the colour of a tape is its region type. Raised white condensed caps with a real emboss, strips cut square or at an angle. Printed pages on white paper. Barlow for reading, Barlow Condensed for tape.

STORY: The visitor sees a site they could own with labels already stuck on it, reads the printout of what was found, flips labels into a form, learns what each colour means, the four steps, six looks, what is not on the shelf, that nothing is charged, then presses one of two tapes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: H1 set on four stacked red tape strips (four, so the longest line fits the column in both languages), the lede, two equal tape-headed actions (waitlist email, invite code). Right: a printed sample site with five tapes stuck to its parts and a label-maker printout of counts beneath. Above the printout, a Labels / Form switch.

FORM: Embossed label tape, position 6 on the re-rolled ordered list, seed key f663443a (re-roll 1). Signature interaction: the Labels / Form switch turns the labelled page into the generated form, CSS-only state with a JS view transition.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
