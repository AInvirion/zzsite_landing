# Surface: zapzapsites.com landing (mockup 01, The Duplicate Order Pad)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: owner-operators of small local businesses, non-technical. Action: two equal actions, join the waitlist or use an invite code. English first with EN/ES switch. Sample businesses only, labelled as samples. Own JS allowed with no-JS fallback. Anti-goals: generic SaaS, corporate, techy, playful.

Open: AI detection is not live in production; the "we work out what should be editable" claim is kept from the current apex copy pending confirmation.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing is a carbonless duplicate order set. You write on the white top sheet; the canary copy underneath is your website. Refuses the category default of headline left, product screenshot right, three feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Sheets are the colour fields: white top sheet, canary #FBE36B copy, pink #F6B3C4 copy, goldenrod third part, a dark chipboard backing. Form rules and labels printed in one spot ink, form green #0B5B45. Filled-in entries in carbon blue #1F3A93 with a faint transfer blur on copies. Archivo, expanded for the printed title, condensed caps for field labels, regular for entries. Perforations and numbered stubs are the controls.

STORY: A visitor sees a form they already understand, sees it become a website on the copy beneath, learns the four steps, sees one order in six looks, learns what is deliberately absent and that nothing is charged, then tears off a stub.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Printed pad header with wordmark, order number and EN/ES. Top sheet across the left 58% carries the H1 as the form title and five ruled fields pre-filled with a labelled sample. The canary copy sits partly beneath it on the right showing the live website render. Along the bottom edge, two equal perforated stubs: Join the waitlist (email) and I have a code.

FORM: Duplicate order pad, position 1 on the ordered list (the pick), seed key f663443a. Signature interaction: typing on the top sheet presses through live to the copy and the six looks.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
