How an order goes through

Four parts, one pass. No repo, no build step, no hosting account, no CSS.

  1. 1 White · Sign in

    Sign in with Google or GitHub. That's the whole account.

  2. 2 Canary · Start

    Pick a template, or bring the site you have: paste it, upload the file or a zip, or give us its address.

  3. 3 Pink · Fill in

    Fill in the form we build from it. Saving publishes, and undo is always there.

  4. 4 Goldenrod · Go live

    Point your domain at us. We show the exact steps for your registrar and watch until it's live. A free zzsites.com address works from minute one.
