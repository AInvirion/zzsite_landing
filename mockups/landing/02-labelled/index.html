Give it a home

On your site, every colour of tape means something

A label says what kind of thing sits under it, and what kind of thing it is decides what you can do with it. Nothing else on the page can be touched, so nothing else can break.

  • Text

    Headlines and paragraphs. Type the words; the layout takes care of itself.

  • Contact

    Phone, email, address. Written once, used everywhere it appears.

  • List

    Questions, services, a menu. Add, remove and reorder the items.

  • Hours

    Opening hours, kept in one place, and readable by search engines.

  • Photo

    Resized for every screen, with the location data stripped out.
